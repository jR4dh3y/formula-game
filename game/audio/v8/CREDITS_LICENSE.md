## Original supplied F430 pack (retained, no longer played)

**IMPORTANT: This pack is not an authentic 2010 F1 recording.** It uses a real recorded 2010 Ferrari F430 racing V8 excerpt as its tonal foundation, pitch-shifted and processed to resemble a high-revving 2006–2013 open-wheel engine. It will not perfectly reproduce a real F1 powertrain. It is a material improvement over a purely sine-wave V8 synthesizer but remains a prototype.

Recording: **“Ferrari F430 (2010)” by Edvvc** (Wikimedia Commons), shared as CC BY-SA 3.0.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ferrari_F430_(2010).ogg

Derived recorded loop preparation: **ssabeeth / v8-engine-explorer** (modified recording, CC BY-SA 3.0).
https://github.com/ssabeeth/v8-engine-explorer

Modifications in this pack: snippet resampled/downmixed/quantized; equalized, crossfaded, looped, replayed at different pitch/RPM, distorted, mixed with original synthesized exhaust turbulence and created mechanical shift sounds.
